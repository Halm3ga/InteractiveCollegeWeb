About FISAT

A campus built for focus & discovery.

Set in the green outskirts of Kochi at Mookkannoor, Angamaly, FISAT blends rigorous academics with clubs, sports, arts and entrepreneurship. Autonomous status means updated, industry-aligned curricula, flexible electives and faster innovation.

To create skilled, ethical engineers and leaders through quality education, research, and strong industry partnership — with personal mentoring for every student.

To be a centre of excellence that shapes technology for society, ranked among India’s most trusted private institutions for learning outcomes and employability.

  • IDEA Lab, CFS & funded research labs

Admissions FAQ

Quick answers. For fee structure and scholarships, talk to our counsellors.

Ask a question
How do I apply for B.Tech 2026?

Apply online, then attend KEAM / management counselling. Our team verifies marks, branch preference and hostel needs within 48 hours.

Is FISAT residential? How is hostel allotted?

Yes — separate men’s and women’s hostels with mess, gym and study halls. Allotment is first-come with distance priority.

What scholarships exist?

Merit (KEAM rank / +2 marks), Federal Bank Officers’ wards, EWS, sports/arts and alumni-funded IDEA grants.

Does autonomous status affect my degree?

No — degree is awarded by KTU. Autonomy lets us update syllabus faster and offer more electives and internships.
